FAQ

Common questions

Do you work with small companies?

Yes. Startups and mid-sized firms often benefit most, because getting the foundation right early saves expensive rework later.

Can you work alongside our existing IT team?

Yes. Most engagements are done with your team so they understand and can maintain everything after handover.

Do you need admin access to our Azure environment?

For reviews, read-only access is usually enough. Build work uses access you control and can remove at any time.

How do you handle confidentiality?

An NDA is signed before any environment details are shared, and client names are never published.
